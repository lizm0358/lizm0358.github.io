About Me

Hello! My name is Liz Mcgregor and I am a first year student in the Interactive Media Design Program at Algonquin College. I am 26 years old and am from Brampton, Ontario. I'm passionate about music and arts and am excited to become more competent in the hands on work that goes into creating a brand and digital creative identity.

I work as a freelance event planner as well as working for a small local events and promotions company called Drowning Fish. We put on concerts, picnics, and themed events of all kinds to try and create accessible and culturally impactful third spaces for the young people of Ottawa.
