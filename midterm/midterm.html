Facts About Starfish

  • Starfish are not real fish because they are invertabrates.
  • They use sea water in their circulatory system instead of blood
  • Starfish have light sensing organs at the end of their limbs.
  • They can live in a variety of different climates, both warm and cold, and can be found everywhere from the Indian and Pacific Ocean to right here in our local waters.
